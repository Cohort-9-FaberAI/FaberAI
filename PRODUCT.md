# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Design engineers and manufacturing engineers reviewing CAD parts during manufacturability checks. They are trying to understand whether a part can be made as designed, why a process may fail, and what changes would improve the result.

## Product Purpose

FaberAI helps users upload CAD files, run geometry and DFM analysis, inspect issues visually, and ask follow-up questions about completed manufacturability reports. Success means an engineer can trust the verdict, trace it to rule evidence, and decide the next design action.

## Brand Personality

Technical, clear, trustworthy. The interface should feel like an engineering review tool: calm, precise, and useful under pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative chat gimmicks, vague AI assistant copy, and visually loud SaaS patterns that distract from the analysis. Do not make the chatbot imply it can inspect raw geometry or recompute results.

## Design Principles

- Explain from evidence: every answer should connect back to report data, rule IDs, and known analysis state.
- Keep the engineer in flow: chat should be available without displacing the model and issue review workflow.
- Make boundaries visible: unavailable reports, missing data, and deterministic fallback behavior should be clear.
- Prefer compact utility over spectacle: controls should be predictable, scannable, and resilient.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, clear error states, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions.
